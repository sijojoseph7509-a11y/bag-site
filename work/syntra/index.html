03 · The problem

Shoppers arrive knowing a mood. The store answers in names.

Names, notes and bottles that all look alike. After a few sprays the nose blurs what the mind is trying to compare, and what should feel personal becomes a guess.

30%

discover perfume by trying in store (YouGov, 2025)

Putting it in their hands.
