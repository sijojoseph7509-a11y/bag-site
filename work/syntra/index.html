My learnings · Syntra

What I learned.

About people

  • Start from a feeling. People know their mood, not the perfume notes.
  • Plain words beat expert words. “Fruity” works, “woody” needs a forest.
  • One clear answer is easier than a wall of choices.

About building

  • Test early, in the real room. Long questions failed in dim light; one-liners worked.
  • Always give an answer. Scoring every scent beat waiting for an exact match.
  • A keepsake needs a nudge. The AR ticket only got scanned once a line by the printer said why.
