Intro to Materials

Three weeks of touching everything: cutting, sanding, bending and assembling, ending in a full menswear fixture and a cap stand for Materially Speaking, in bamboo, folding plywood, MDF and Canadian wood.

Key learnings
  1. You learn a material with your hands: cutting, sanding and bending taught more than any sample board.
  2. Let each material do the job it is good at: bamboo bends, folding plywood folds, MDF and Canadian wood hold the structure.
