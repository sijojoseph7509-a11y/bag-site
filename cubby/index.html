Design Project 02 · Semester 03 · 2026

Cubby

A home for your helmet. Safe helmet storage where riders actually park: mall basements and cinemas. Scan a QR, book a Cubby, ride light. Eight weeks of research at Phoenix, Mantri and Orion, through to a working prototype.

My learnings · Cubby

If I started again…

Learned

  • Measure first. My first box was guessed, and wrong.
  • Count, don’t just observe: log n for every interview.
  • Prototype the lock earlier; it changed the band, the bay and the height.
  • Design for the basement: no signal, low light, dust.

Next

  1. Bench-test the Tronic lock; set the keeper hole.
  2. Measure 10–15 more helmets with photo proof.
  3. 8–12 structured interviews and a 2-hour helmet count.
  4. Apply the 0.045 m band to Bay Primary and Foyer.
  5. Supplier quotes on the same drawings.
