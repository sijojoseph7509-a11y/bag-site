My learnings · Ather Energy

Ather sells an ecosystem. Its stores still sell a scooter.

Reading the brand as one system (market, supply chain, money, stores) showed me where the experience breaks, and that a small, cheap touchpoint can fix more than a big one.

The showroom undersells the product

Both Bengaluru stores we visited gave the hero scooter no hero treatment: no lighting, elevation or story.

Accessories are a business, not an add-on

Non-vehicle revenue is already 13–14% for Ather. A small Halo kiosk can grow it and capture test-ride leads.

Good retail is a system

Product, information, charging and service have to sit together, in store and online.
