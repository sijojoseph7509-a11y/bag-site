My learnings · Ergonomics

IKEA fits the body. It strains the mind.

A space can pass every reach and aisle check and still tire people out. The forced route, the switch into the warehouse and prices placed away from the product all add load. Ergonomics is the body and the mind together.

Design for the full range

Not the average body: standing, seated, child and elderly.

Keep information close

Price and details on the product cut decision time.

Give people a way out

Visible shortcuts and rest points lower route anxiety.
